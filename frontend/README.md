# WellPilot

## AI-Powered Wellness & Lifestyle Intelligence Platform

### Wellness Hackathon 2026 Project

---

## Overview

WellPilot is an AI-powered wellness platform that helps users improve their physical and mental well-being through personalized wellness insights, AI coaching, lifestyle intelligence, wellness challenges, and resort experience recommendations.

The platform combines Artificial Intelligence, Wellness Technology, and Hospitality Experiences to create a personalized wellness ecosystem that helps users align their daily habits with their biology.

### Tagline

**Align Your Daily Habits with Your Biology**

---

## Problem Statement

Modern wellness solutions are often:

* Generic and one-size-fits-all
* Expensive and inaccessible
* Difficult to maintain consistently
* Focused on short-term results
* Lacking continuous support

Many students, professionals, and travelers struggle with:

* Stress and burnout
* Poor sleep habits
* Low physical activity
* Poor hydration
* Lack of personalized wellness guidance

People do not need more wellness information. They need personalized, intelligent guidance that fits their lifestyle.

---

## Solution

WellPilot provides a complete wellness ecosystem that:

1. Assesses a user's wellness condition.
2. Calculates a personalized wellness score.
3. Uses AI to analyze wellness patterns.
4. Provides personalized recommendations.
5. Suggests wellness challenges.
6. Recommends resort and wellness experiences.
7. Tracks wellness progress over time.

---

## Core Features

### Wellness Assessment

Users complete a wellness assessment covering:

* Stress Level
* Sleep Quality
* Hydration
* Physical Activity
* Mood

Output:

* Wellness Score
* Wellness Category
* Personalized Insights

---

### AI Wellness Coach

An AI-powered assistant that provides:

* Stress management guidance
* Sleep improvement suggestions
* Productivity recommendations
* Hydration reminders
* Healthy lifestyle coaching

Supports:

* English
* Amharic

---

### Wellness Dashboard

Provides users with:

* Wellness Score
* Progress Tracking
* Wellness Statistics
* Activity Trends
* Challenge Performance

---

### Wellness Challenges

Users can participate in wellness challenges such as:

* Hydration Challenge
* Better Sleep Challenge
* Walking Challenge
* Stress-Free Week Challenge

Features:

* Challenge Completion Tracking
* Wellness Points
* Daily Streaks
* Reflection Journal
* Progress Statistics

---

### Personalized Resort Recommendations

WellPilot recommends wellness experiences based on:

* Wellness Score
* Stress Level
* Sleep Quality
* Activity Level
* Hydration Level
* Mood

Examples:

High Stress:

* Spa Therapy
* Meditation
* Nature Walks

Poor Sleep:

* Sleep Wellness Programs
* Evening Meditation

Low Activity:

* Swimming

* Fitness Programs

Excellent Wellness:

* Adventure Activities
* Social Wellness Events
